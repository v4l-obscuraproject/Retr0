The Reality of Public "Hacking" Tools

Many free DDoS tools and RAT software shared on Discord servers and forums are genuine weapons used in cyberattacks. The true hacker is the creator of these tools; you are not the hacker.

You must make a conscious choice before executing such programs. Unlike phishing emails, there was no deception here; you chose to initiate an action that could damage a computer. Just like in cyberattacks, running this type of software requires careful decision-making, and most countries classify such actions as criminal behavior.

How do legitimate red teams operate differently?

In accordance with signed agreements, standard security testing allows experts to assess specific systems. This process is not based on exceptions, so the reasoning of "I wanted to see how it works" does not justify the actions of a security professional in these situations.
